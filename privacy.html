Privacy Policy

Timely · Last updated 31 October 2026

Timely is a personal planner for tracking savings goals, school work and errands. It is built and run by Eric Gathige Kariuki in Kenya. This page explains what information Timely keeps and how it is used.

What Timely stores

  • Account details: your email address, and the name you choose when you first open the app. If you sign in with Google, Timely receives only your name and email address from Google.
  • What you enter: your savings goals and deposits, school items (CATs, assignments, exams, lectures), errands and any notes you add.
  • On your device only: your profile picture, light or dark choice and reminder settings never leave your device.

How it is used

Your information is used only to sign you in and to keep your data in sync across your own devices. Timely has no advertising and no tracking, and your information is never sold or shared for marketing.

Where it is kept

Your synced data is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in service Timely uses. Google handles the Google sign-in step. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each person can only read and change their own data.

Who can see it

Other users cannot see your data. As the person running the service, I can technically reach the database, but I will not read your personal entries except to fix a problem you report or where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email me at the address below and I will do it within 30 days. You can also use Export in the app to save a copy of your data first, and you can clear the data on a device by clearing the site data in your browser.

Children

Timely is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
